- BP, O2

## Head & Neck
- **Eye**：Pale conjunctiva, icteric sclera, exophthalmos, lid lag, ptosis
- **Ear**：Tragus tenderness, canal erythema, membrane bulging, membrane perforation
- **Nose**：Mucosal edema, purulent discharge, sinus tenderness
- **Mouth**：Dentition, oral ulcer, tonsil swelling, tonsil exudate
- **Thyroid**：Goiter, nodule, tenderness, texture, bruit
- **Lymph**：Submental, submandibular, parotid, pre-auricular, post-auricular, occipital, anterior cervical, posterior cervical, supraclavicular
- **Vessel**：EJV engorgement, JVP height, hepatojugular reflux, carotid bruit

## Chest
- **Inspection**：Zoster, inhalation (symmetry, accessory muscle, sternal retraction), shape (barrel, funnel, pigeon)
- **Palpation (lung)**：Trachea deviation, chest expansion, tactile fremitus
- **Palpation (heart)**：PMI, thrill, heave
- **Percussion (lung)**：Hyperresonance, dullness (anterior + posterior)
- **Auscultation (lung)**：Rhonchi, wheeze, coarse crackle, fine crackle (anterior + posterior)
- **Auscultation (heart)**：Arrhythmia, gallop, murmur (2nd-right-sternal: A, 2nd-left-sternal: P, 4nd-left-sternal: T, 5nd-mid-calvicular: M)

## Abdomen
- **Inspection**：Distension, hernia, caput medusae, spider angioma
- **Auscultation**：Bowel sound, bruits
- **Percussion**：Tympany, dullness, ascites (fluid wave, shifting dullness)
- **Percussion (hepatobiliary)**：Liver span, spleen span
- **Palpation**：Tenderness (McBurney, Murphy), rebound tenderness, muscle guarding, mass
- **Palpation (hepatobiliary)**：Hepatomegaly, splenomegaly
- **Test**：Obturator, Psoas, Rovsing

## Limb & Back
- **Inspection**：Rash, deformation
- **Inspection (circulation)**：Pallor, cyanosis, clubbing
- **Inspection (inflammation)**：Erythema, swelling
- **Palpation (circulation)**：Edema, pulse, CRT
- **Palpation (inflammation)**：Warmth, tenderness
- **Percussion**：CVA tenderness
- **Test (general)**：ROM, MMT
- **Test (upper limb)**：Tinel, Phalen, Finkelstein
- **Test (lower limb)**：Drawer, stress, McMurray
- **Test (spine)**：Spurling, SLRT, FABER
- **Test (nerve)**：Asterixis, pronator drift

## Neurologic
- **CN I**：Smelling (scent)
- **CN II**：Visual field (left-up/right-down, left-down/right-up), pupillary light reflex (direct, indirect)
- **CN III, IV, VI**：Lateral (LR), lateral-up (SR), lateral-down (IR), medial (MR), medial-up (IO), medial-down (SO), convergence
- **CN V**：Facial sensation (V1, V2, V3), mastication
- **CN VII**：Facial motor (forehead wrinkle, close eye, show teeth, purse lip)
- **CN VIII**：Hearing (Weber, Rinne)
- **CN IX, X**：Uvula deviation (away from lesion side), palate no elevation (lesion side)
- **CN XI**：SCM (turn head), trapezius (shrug shoulder)
- **CN XII**：Tongue protrusion (to lesion side)
- **Motor**：Atrophy, tone, strength (shoulder, elbow, wrist, hand, hip, knee, ankle)
- **Sensory**：Touch, pain, vibration/proprioception (hand, forearm, arm, foot, leg, thigh)
- **Reflex**：DTR (biceps, triceps, knee, ankle), Babinski, Hoffmann
- **Coordination**：Finger-nose-finger, rapid alternating movement, gait (Romberg, tandem walk)
- **Test**：Nuchal rigidity, Kernig, Brudzinski
